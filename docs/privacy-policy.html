Privacy Policy — Deutsch mit Brezel

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Deutsch mit Brezel ("the App") is a self-hosted, single-operator tool used to create German language-learning videos and publish them to the operator's own social media accounts on YouTube, Instagram, and Facebook. This policy explains what data the App accesses, how it is used, and how it is stored. The App is operated privately by an individual and is not offered as a public service.

1. Who this policy covers

The App is used by a single operator (its owner) to publish content that the operator creates. It does not have public end users, does not collect information from visitors, and does not process data belonging to anyone other than the operator's own connected accounts.

2. Information the App accesses

To publish videos, the App connects to platform APIs on the operator's behalf and accesses only:

  • Account access tokens issued by Google (YouTube Data API) and Meta (Facebook Graph API / Instagram Graph API) after the operator explicitly signs in and grants permission.
  • Basic account identifiers needed to publish — for example the connected YouTube channel, the linked Instagram Business account ID, and the Facebook Page ID and name.
  • The video files and captions/metadata that the operator creates within the App and chooses to upload.

The App does not access private messages, contacts, followers' data, or content belonging to other users.

3. How the information is used

Accessed data is used for one purpose only: to publish the operator's own videos to the operator's own YouTube, Instagram, and Facebook accounts, including optional features such as scheduling a post or setting a video thumbnail. The data is not used for advertising, profiling, or analytics.

4. Storage and security

  • Access tokens and credentials are stored locally on the operator's own computer (in local credential files such as token.json, instagram_creds.json, and facebook_creds.json, and in a local .env file).
  • These files are excluded from version control and are never uploaded, published, or transmitted to any third party by the App.
  • The App communicates only with the official Google and Meta API endpoints required to publish content.

5. Data sharing

The App does not sell, rent, or share any accessed data with third parties. The only external parties involved are the platforms the operator publishes to (Google/YouTube and Meta/Facebook/Instagram), whose own privacy policies govern the content once it is published.

6. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored tokens remain until the operator deletes them. The operator can revoke the App's access at any time by clearing the stored credentials in the App (the "Reset" controls on its Connections page) and by removing the App's access from Google Account permissions and Facebook Business Integrations. Deleting the local credential files removes all stored access data.

7. Cookies and analytics

This privacy policy page and the App do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics services, and do not collect information from visitors to this page.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to: deutschmitbrezel@gmail.com
